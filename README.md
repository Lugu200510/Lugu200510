## 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm **Zhenshuo Zhang**, an Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the **University of Southampton**. I'm interested in **digital IC design and verification, FPGA systems, and embedded hardware**, with hands-on experience in RTL development, silicon testing, and system-level debugging.

## 💼 Internship Experience

### Digital IC Design & Verification Intern · Jun–Aug 2026
*Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area Institute of Integrated Circuits and System Applications*

- Designed and verified **APB-UART, dual-FIFO, a simplified multi-cycle MIPS processor, and a small SoC** using Verilog and self-checking testbenches.
- Used **VCS and Verdi** for simulation, waveform debugging, and code/functional coverage analysis.

### Robotics Test Intern · Jul–Sep 2025
*Guangzhou LiGong Industrial Co., Ltd. · R&D Department*

- Tested **AMR localization repeatability and vision-guided docking**, including 50+ guidance trials and accuracy measurements.
- Validated **ROS-based navigation, LiDAR obstacle avoidance, EtherCAT status, and safety functions**.

## 🔧 Selected Projects

- **Digital IC Tape-Out & Testing:** Implemented and verified a rotary-encoder-related IC using **S-Edit, L-Edit, and Calibre (DRC/LVS/PEX)**; the fabricated chip passed hardware tests.
- **FPGA FIR Filter:** Built a **16-tap, 16-bit FIR filter** in Verilog with a sequential MAC datapath; verified with **Quartus, ModelSim, and DE1-SoC**.
- **Smart Stadium Seat Node:** Developed **Pico 2 embedded LCD/UI functions** for a team system with sensors and **RS-485** communication.
