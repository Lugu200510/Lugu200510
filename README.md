## 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm **Zhenshuo Zhang**, an Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the **University of Southampton**. My main interests are **digital IC design and verification, FPGA development, and embedded systems**. Through internships and hardware projects, I've worked on RTL verification, IC implementation, FPGA signal processing, and robotic system testing.

## 💼 Internship Experience

### Digital IC Design & Verification Intern | Jun–Aug 2026
*Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area Institute of Integrated Circuits and System Applications · Digital Group*

- Designed and verified **APB-UART, dual-FIFO modules, a simplified multi-cycle MIPS processor, and a small SoC**, using RTL and self-checking testbenches.
- Used **Synopsys VCS and Verdi** for simulation, waveform debugging, and coverage analysis; extended reset, exception, and boundary tests to achieve **100% code and functional coverage** in the reported verification work.
- Studied **DDR4/DDR5/LPDDR6** memory interface IP and Controller/PHY architectures.

### Robotics Test Intern | Jul–Sep 2025
*Guangzhou LiGong Industrial Co., Ltd. · R&D Department*

- Evaluated **AMR fixed-path repeatability** across **50 measurements** (5 points × 10 runs), recording approximately **60 mm** repeatability with a laser tracker.
- Conducted **50+ vision-guidance accuracy tests**, measuring approximately **3 mm** guidance repeatability; verified heading deviation of **<1°** for precise docking.
- Tested **ROS navigation, automatic charging, LiDAR obstacle avoidance, and safety functions**, and investigated EtherCAT and robot-arm motor-state issues.

## 🔧 Selected Engineering Projects

- **Multi-Module IC Design, Tape-Out & Post-Silicon Verification:** Contributed to a team chip comprising a ring oscillator, sequence recognizer, 4-bit adder, and human–machine interface; personally worked on the **4-bit serial-carry adder and up/down counter**. Used **S-Edit, L-Edit, Calibre (DRC/LVS/PEX), and ModelSim**; the team's chip completed **TSMC tape-out and post-silicon verification**.
- **Parameterized FIFO Verification:** Developed a **SystemVerilog self-checking testbench** with a queue-based reference model, assertions, and coverage to identify **full/empty, underflow, and simultaneous read/write** defects.
- **16-Tap FPGA FIR Filter:** Implemented an **FSM-controlled sequential MAC FIR filter** with 16-bit datapath arithmetic in **SystemVerilog**, verified in **ModelSim/Quartus** and on **DE1-SoC (Cyclone V)**; verified a fixed **19-clock-cycle latency** from `input_ready` to `output_ready`.
- **SAFEGOALS Smart Stadium Seat Node:** Led the **ILI9341 SPI LCD and UI** portion of a **Raspberry Pi Pico 2** team prototype, integrating dashboards, voting, temperature alerts, emergency modes, and **RS-485** communication.
