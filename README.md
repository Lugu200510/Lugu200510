## 👋 About Me

Hi there! I'm **Zhenshuo Zhang**, an Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the **University of Southampton** in the UK.

I enjoy building systems where **digital hardware meets intelligent computing**. My interests range from **Verilog RTL design and FPGA signal processing** to **IC implementation, embedded interfaces, and hardware for machine learning**. What I find most rewarding is taking an idea beyond a simulation: designing it, verifying it, and seeing it work on real hardware.

My engineering experience includes a **fabricated and successfully tested IC**, an **FPGA-based FIR filter**, and **embedded team projects**. I'm now exploring how spiking neural networks can be mapped onto digital hardware for biomedical signal classification.

> **Engineering interests:** FPGA · Digital IC Design & Verification · Embedded Systems · Neuromorphic Computing · AI Hardware

## 🛠️ Selected Engineering Projects

### 🔬 Digital IC Design & Tape-out — Completed
- Worked on a **rotary-encoder-related IC** through schematic/layout design and physical verification.
- Experience with **S-Edit, L-Edit, DRC, LVS, PEX, and GDSII**.
- The chip was **fabricated and successfully tested**.

### ⚡ FPGA FIR Filter — Completed
- Developed a **16-tap, 16-bit FIR architecture** using a sequential multiply-accumulate (MAC) datapath and FSM control.
- Used **Verilog**, **Intel Quartus**, and **ModelSim**, with the **DE1-SoC** FPGA platform.
- Focused on fixed-point arithmetic, latency, control logic, and simulation-based verification.

### 📟 Embedded Seat-Node System — Team Project
- Built an embedded node around **Raspberry Pi Pico W / Pico 2** microcontrollers.
- Integrated an **SPI display, keypad, temperature and motion-related sensors, emergency controls, and RS-485 communication**.
- Worked on embedded firmware, peripheral interfaces, and reliable team-level communication.

### 🤖 Reinforcement Learning for Robot Navigation — Exploration / Reproduction
- Exploring **DQN / TD3-based autonomous navigation** in simulated environments.
- Working with **Python, reinforcement learning workflows, and ROS/Gazebo-related tooling**.
- Focus: understanding, reproducing, and evaluating learning-based navigation methods.

## 🧠 Current Research

**FPGA-Based Spiking Neural Network for Biomedical Signal Classification** — *Final-year project, ongoing*

**Supervisor:** Prof. Koushik Maharatna · University of Southampton

The planned research pipeline is:

`Public biomedical dataset → PC preprocessing & training → FPGA-based SNN inference → Classification output`

I'm investigating **Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) neurons**, **surrogate-gradient training**, and practical trade-offs in implementing SNN inference on FPGA hardware. **FPGA deployment and experimental results are project goals, not claimed completed outcomes.**
